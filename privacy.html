Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

"Nimrod's Personal Assistant" is a private tool used only by its owner, Nimrod Nagy, with his own Google account.

What data it accesses

With the owner's consent, the tool accesses his Gmail messages and drafts and his Google Calendar events through the Google APIs, in order to read, search, draft and send email and to view and edit calendar events on his behalf.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to perform the actions the owner asks for.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computers.
  • The data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The data is not used to train generalised AI or machine learning models.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
